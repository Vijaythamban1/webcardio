WebCardio engagement · Notice dated 28 October 2026

Privacy and confidentiality

This notice describes how Vijay and Sujeesh intend to handle information shared for the WebCardio discovery and improvement engagement. It applies to role details, nominations, short preparation inputs and workshop contributions. The facilitators should confirm these arrangements with WebCardio before collecting responses through any additional service.

Purpose and limits on use

Information is to be used only to understand WebCardio’s working practices, plan and facilitate the sessions, and develop recommendations or solutions for WebCardio. It must not be reused for unrelated engagements, marketing, public case studies, research publication or model training without separate explicit authorization.

What we ask for

A first name or working name, role/community, responsibilities, relevant application or workflow, nominations and short observations. Tools, a brief lifecycle description and additional comments are optional. Full legal names, patient records, identifiable clinical data, credentials, access tokens and confidential source code are not required and should not be included.

What this website does

The preparation forms run in the participant’s browser and generate a downloadable text file. They do not submit answers to a server or create a shared response database. There is no public participant directory or response listing. Answers remain in the page until it is closed or reloaded, subject to normal browser behavior. The downloaded file remains on the participant’s device until they delete it.

The participant or coordinator must send the file directly to Vijay or Sujeesh through an agreed private channel. The website cannot verify who receives that file or automatically confirm delivery. Its public briefing, source code and this notice are publicly accessible through GitHub Pages; participant responses are not committed to the repository.

Access and confidentiality

Identifiable preparation responses and nominations are intended for Vijay and Sujeesh as facilitators. They will not publish these responses on the site or disclose named individual comments to the wider team without the contributor’s agreement. A finalized attendee list can be shared for session coordination as agreed. Findings should be reported as themes or aggregated observations where practical, and participants should be told if a specific example could identify them.

Tools and processing

Hosting, browsers, private communication channels and workshop tools necessarily process data under their own terms and settings. Therefore this notice does not claim that no processing occurs anywhere. Before using another service to collect, store, record, transcribe or analyze responses, the facilitators should explain the service, access and purpose and obtain the appropriate agreement.

Preparation responses should not be uploaded to external AI tools for analysis unless WebCardio explicitly authorizes the tools, purpose and handling in advance. This notice does not assert that past conversations were never processed or that third-party services provide unverified guarantees.

Workshop participation

Identified planning inputs are separate from session contributions. Before the workshop, the facilitators will explain whether names are visible, whether any anonymous-writing feature is available, who can access the Mural board and whether anything is recorded. No anonymity or recording consent should be assumed from completing a preparation form.

Retention and requests

Keep identifiable inputs only for as long as needed for this engagement. Agree the retention period and deletion point with WebCardio before collecting through a centralized system. At engagement close, delete raw responses unless a specific continuation has been agreed. Public summaries should not contain personal responses.

Contact Vijay or Sujeesh through the same agreed communication channel to correct an answer, request its removal, withdraw an identifiable quote or ask about access. Requests should be addressed before further use where feasible; already distributed files or third-party copies may require separate action.

Acknowledgement

The form acknowledgement confirms that the contributor has read this notice and understands the private return process. It does not authorize unrelated use, publication, recording or AI analysis. Any additional confidentiality agreement or WebCardio organizational requirement should be agreed separately and takes precedence where applicable.
